# Third-party notices

Ren includes, or is used alongside, the third-party software below.

- __Prism__ 1.29.0 is built into `ren.js`, from `vendor/prism.js`, to highlight SQL and
  JavaScript.  It's been customised (its SQL keywords suit SQL Server), and it's under the MIT
  licence.
- __d3__ 7.9.0 isn't included any more, but the charts' scales, tick choices and curves
  (`Ren.ChartScales` and `Ren.ChartPaths`) are ported from its algorithms, so its notice stays.
  It's under the ISC licence.
- __write-excel-file__ 1.4.27 isn't built into `ren.js`, but a page needs to include it before
  it.  A copy is in `examples/lib/` for the examples and tests.  It's under the MIT licence.
- __Newtonsoft.Json__ 13.0.3 is a dependency of the .NET package, `Ren`, rather than being
  included in it.  It's under the MIT licence.

Their licences follow.
